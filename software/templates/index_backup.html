About these readings

Initial values and generated readings are simulated, not measurements from your sensors. The thresholds are provisional demonstration settings. Turbidity and TDS cannot independently establish drinking-water safety. The DHT22 measures ambient temperature, not water temperature. Sensor calibration and suitable water-quality testing are required for meaningful real-world results.
